Data Sources

Every number in the observatory traces to one of these sources. Reliability tier reflects methodology, institutional mandate, and independence from the data subjects.

What reliability tiers mean:  Official Primary — statutory agency collecting the data directly. Official Secondary — compiled from official records by a credible body. Credible Secondary — reported by a credible outlet citing primary data, unconfirmed directly. Unverified — estimate with no stated methodology; shown for completeness only.
